Imagine — One Message. One Feeling.
The biggest mistake in safety communication? Trying to say everything.
Sticky messages say one thing — unforgettably.
Made to Stick: The Curse of Knowledge kills most safety comms. You know too much — so you say too much. The antidote: strip your message to its single irreducible core. One sentence. One emotion. One action.
⚡ Before & After — Safety Alert
❌ Before (typical alert)
"Following the recordable incident on 14/03, a full root cause analysis has been conducted. Contributing factors include inadequate pre-task planning, failure to identify hazard controls in the JSEA, and non-compliance with PPE requirements under Section 4.2 of the HSE Management Plan..."
✅ After (Made to Stick)
One worker went home injured last Tuesday. Here's what happened in 47 seconds — and the one thing we're all changing from today.

30-second animated replay. One clear change. Shared across all sites same day.
🌿 Before & After — ESG Report
❌ Before (typical ESG)
"In FY2024, the organisation achieved a 12.4% reduction in Scope 2 emissions against the 2019 baseline, with water consumption per tonne of ore processed declining by 8.7%..."
✅ After (visual story)
We saved enough water this year to fill 47 Olympic swimming pools. Here's how our crew made it happen — in 90 seconds.

Animated data story. Human faces. Numbers people feel, not just read.
